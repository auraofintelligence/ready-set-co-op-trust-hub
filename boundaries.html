Care and permission

Trust grows when the boundary is visible.

The hub can invite participation without exposing private lives, sensitive business details, cultural material, legal risk or speculative work that belongs deeper in the pathway.

Boundary map

What belongs where.

Use this as a public checklist before publishing pages, photos, videos, grant notes or asset lists.

PublicSafe to publish

Public service descriptions, approved event photos, public contact channels, open repo links, dates checked, role types and consented acknowledgements.

PrivateKeep in working files

Phone numbers, addresses, emergency details, logins, raw interview notes, private finances, unapproved contacts and exact storage locations.

CulturalNeeds custodial review

Traditional knowledge, language content, sacred sites, cultural protocols, identity claims and any material that belongs under Quandamooka-controlled processes.

LegalInformation, not advice

Source trails can collect official links and dates. Legal interpretation, contracts, labour hire, RTO, property and co-op decisions need qualified review.

CommercialVerify before claims

Lease prices, purchase prices, licences, insurance, revenue projections, grant eligibility and employment claims must be checked close to use.

SpeculativeKeep the horizon optional

Mineral Moonshots, subterranean systems, digital twins and future civilisation material should sit behind grounded skills and trust steps.

Public tone

Warm, grounded, and brave enough to say "not yet".

The site should not sand the vision down into a generic business page. It should keep the heart: joyful responsible abundance, local care, jobs, trust and future capability.

But it should also protect people from vertigo. Lead with useful work, plain evidence, careful media, clear roles and honest status notes. Let the deep layers be available for people who choose to keep travelling.

Public claim rule

If a claim would require legal, property, funding, cultural, medical, safety or government verification, say it as a plan or possibility until the relevant authority confirms it.

Before publishing

A small checklist saves trust later.

ConsentDo people know where the media will appear?

Get clear permission for identifiable people, children, workplaces, private property or personal stories.

AccuracyAre dates, prices and statuses current?

Property listings, grants, public officials, rules and leases can change quickly.

PrivacyCould this expose someone?

Remove private screens, raw contacts, exact gear storage, financial details and anything that could invite harm.

CultureDoes this need First Nations review?

When it touches Country, language, cultural material or authority, slow down and ask the right people.

LegalsIs this legal advice by accident?

Link to official sources and suggest qualified help. Do not present an AI/source note as legal advice.

FitIs this the right page?

Front door pages stay grounded. Deep future material goes to the horizon page or Mineral Moonshots.
