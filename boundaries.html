Care and permission

Trust grows when the boundary is visible.

The hub can invite participation without exposing private lives, sensitive business details, cultural material, legal risk or optional future work that belongs at the right depth.

Boundary map

What belongs where.

Use this as a public checklist before publishing pages, photos, videos, grant notes or asset lists.

PublicSafe to publish

Public service descriptions, approved event photos, public contact channels, open repo links, dates checked, role types and consented acknowledgements.

PrivateKeep in working files

Phone numbers, addresses, emergency details, logins, raw interview notes, private finances, unapproved contacts and exact storage locations.

CulturalNeeds custodial review

Traditional knowledge, language content, sacred sites, cultural protocols, identity claims and any material that belongs under Quandamooka-controlled processes.

LegalInformation, not advice

Source trails can collect official links and dates. Legal interpretation, contracts, labour hire, RTO, property and co-op decisions need qualified review.

CommercialVerify before claims

Lease prices, purchase prices, licences, insurance, revenue projections, grant eligibility and employment claims must be checked close to use.

SpeculativeKeep the horizon optional

Mineral Moonshots, subterranean systems, digital twins and future civilisation material stay connected to grounded skills, trust and consent.
