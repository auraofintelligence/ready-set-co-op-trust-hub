Help with organising and responsibilities

Know what needs doing, and who can help.

A hall booking, a film night or a shared piece of equipment comes with details someone has to look after. Permission, insurance, safe arrangements and a clear contact all help an activity run smoothly.

Ready SETCO proposes sharing that organising work through a local team. Bring the activity you have in mind, so we can work through what it needs and keep the useful records together.

This support is being developed with interested people. The organiser for each activity would remain responsible for its arrangements and any specialist advice it needs.

For each activity

Work through the details together.

A small workshop and a public screening need different preparation. Starting with the place, the people and what they will be doing helps the organiser check the right requirements with the venue, relevant authority, insurer or qualified adviser.

People and responsibility

Name the organiser, legal operator, activity lead and contact person. Agree the scope, budget, supervision and response when something changes.

Place and permissions

Confirm venue permission, access, accessibility, capacity and any relevant event, screening, music or public-space requirements.

Insurance and safe delivery

Check the activities and people actually covered, the venue's requirements, equipment arrangements, weather plan and emergency contacts.

Work and learning

Set out paid duties, learning arrangements and any voluntary contribution separately. Confirm the employer or provider and the conditions before work starts.

Information and consent

Explain what details are collected, why they are needed, who can see them and how to request a correction. Ask separately about public stories and images.

Evidence and follow-up

Keep permissions, checks, decisions and review dates together. Record incidents and improvements, and close out grant or venue reporting where agreed.

Your information

Share what is useful, with permission.

A notice about an activity can help the whole community, while the contact details and paperwork behind it stay with the people who need them. You should know what is being collected, who can see it and how to ask for a correction.

You choose what to share. Cultural knowledge, language and information about Country remain subject to the relevant custodians' authority and permissions.

Read the wider proposals for community control of information and fair connections between systems.

A clear source trail

Record the source, date checked, person responsible and next review date. Show whether a statement is a proposal, a confirmed arrangement or an update awaiting verification.

For notices used during a disruption, keep the original issuer and issue time visible. Emergency directions come from the responsible authorities.
