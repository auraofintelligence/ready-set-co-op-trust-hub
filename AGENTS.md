# Ready SETCO working instructions

Read [the accepted rebuild brief](docs/REBUILD-BRIEF.md), README.md and any relevant operating documentation before changing this site. The brief records Luke's direction agreed on 19 November 2026. Current human instructions govern new work where earlier research differs.

## Purpose and scope

- Write for local residents, businesses, clubs and community groups with no prior knowledge of Luke or this project. Introduce the full name, Ready Sustainable Employment and Training Cooperative, near the top of the homepage. Explain its practical purpose, proposed/member-shaped status and how people can take part before wider framework language. Introduce Luke and Strange but True by their role; do not assume familiarity. Keep the deeper Sensorium, fractal and superintelligence framing in the background and About material.
- Every public page is a possible first encounter with Luke's work. Explain its topic, local usefulness, present stage and next step in ordinary language before introducing project names or wider research. Explain the “missing middle” as the people, training, equipment, funding and coordination needed to turn an idea into practical work. Keep the wider web available through contextual links; never write public pages as design instructions about what visitors should understand.
- Explain unfamiliar ideas inline through natural, connected prose about familiar local needs. Do not turn an audience clarification into a glossary or headings such as “What is a minimum viable community?” and “What is the missing middle?”. Introduce a project's name after its practical purpose when useful. Keep genuine form prompts, work enquiries and practical help questions where they serve the task.
- Keep the writing mostly descriptive, with “you” in personal invitations and “we/us” at important moments of shared purpose, ownership, decisions and building together. Write “Together, we”, not “Together, they”, when the invitation includes us. Do not turn every factual paragraph into first person or imply existing membership, legal authority or endorsement; facts about outside parties and original operators keep their proper subjects.
- Provide a clear backpacker/working holiday route from the homepage through jobs.html#backpacker-work to the enquiry form. Explain that roles are developing, verify current Home Affairs rules and date-sensitive changes, keep paid employment separate from voluntary C-Hours, and never guarantee visa qualification. Preserve the distinct working_holiday interest through private intake and review; do not preselect consent.
- Ready SETCO is a proposed **minimum viable community for a fair go in the age of superintelligence**. “Fractal” describes a repeatable foundation with locally chosen priorities and peer relationships.
- Lead with events, training and work, notices/news/media, grants, compliance support and member decisions. Keep **Map** in the main menu and mobile navigation. `map.html` is geographic; `site-map.html` is the site directory.
- Let pages read as a calm, guided introduction. The existing Previous/Next footer is the main reading sequence; keep the menu for direct access. Avoid repeated introductory button pairs, generic jump-down prompts and multiple competing invitations to the same destination. Keep the service overview, useful contextual links and specific task routes, with optional research clearly secondary. Preserve map, form and accessibility controls. This is a navigation adjustment, not a reduction of the cooperative's purpose or a forced linear journey.
- Keep the opening centred on sustainable employment and training, with this explicit audience order: local businesses, community organisations and community leaders are being invited as cooperative cofounders, including clubs, the cultural and health sectors and their paid workers and volunteers; then the wider community and backpackers; then tourists. Everyone has a place, with different roles and priorities. Do not shrink that founding invitation to businesses and sports clubs or treat businesses merely as customers or staffing clients. Paid work, volunteering and training belong together in the cooperative's purpose, while their arrangements and records remain distinct. Events, shared resources and practical support connect that work.
- Within work and training, locals and backpackers are both catered for, with neither group prioritised. Lead with the shared offer and keep specific working-holiday guidance easy to reach within it. The island's small year-round community and much larger holiday visitor economy need to work together: frame tourism as supporting local livelihoods and community life, with locally shaped activities and services. Do not introduce unverified population figures or visitor headcounts into the public copy.
- Keep the map narrow and simple across ages: find a place, add a place, suggest a correction. Optional details sit behind progressive disclosure. The wider Sensorium informs compatible records; it does not expand this app into self/home/bioregion portals, a governance engine or a task manager. Read [the growth constraints](docs/SENSORIUM-GROWTH.md).
- Map contribution has two lanes: a quick pin that needs no typing (optional name, then Send pin), and an explicitly chosen detailed entry. An empty-map tap opens the quick card, never the full form. Do not require a category, icon, source questionnaire or a long form before a quick pin can be sent. The send action clearly offers the pin for public-map review; only a confirmed durable save reports success, and publication still needs review. Keep uncertain quick-send retries on the same UUID and payload.
- The Straddie Noticeboard Network is the first proposed shared build. Embed the everyday, disruption and recovery capabilities of the current Aura Disaster Resilience work. The latest media gateway is Minjerribah Screen & Media Network.
- Members choose the cooperative's projects. Distinguish preparatory research, proposals, agreed commitments and operating services. Connected websites and researched organisations do not establish assent, membership, partnership, funding, venue access or installations.
- Keep earlier Trust Hub, property and horizon material available as clearly marked research. Preserve original documents, source identities and build history.

## People, records and handover

- Strange But True is the initial sole trader operator. Ready SETCO remains proposed until its actual legal status changes. Expressions of interest are separate from formal admission, employment and service agreements.
- Record a handover's original operator, receiving legal entity, scope, consent and effective date. Preserve the original record and audit history. Never silently relabel earlier obligations or participants as belonging to a future entity.
- One C-hour recognises one verified hour of **voluntary human contribution**, with no monetary equivalent. Keep paid work, investment, money, machine time/output and outcomes in distinct records. C-Hours do not replace wages.
- People control their private Auras and choose sources and sharing. Do not default to compulsory timesheets, public profiles, raw private records or automatic wider sharing. Cultural authority and permissions remain with the relevant custodians.

## Design and implementation

- Design for phones first. Body text is at least 16px; interactive targets are at least 44px. Check narrow screens, keyboard access, visible focus, safe areas and reduced motion. Keep useful content available without JavaScript.
- Preserve highlighted Strange But True purple, blue, yellow and green. Use the tide favicon family; retain its provenance. Label generated concepts and distinguish them from real places or operating facilities.
- Load the external map only after the visitor chooses to load it. Keep direct area links usable. Never request geolocation automatically or expose private participant locations.
- Public place suggestions need a confirmed save and a human publication decision. Preserve stable IDs, original reports, source dates, permission and correction history; public exports include only published information. Historical research and old map pins stay reference material until checked for this collection. Base-map data and the locally controlled place register are separate sources.
- Keep the existing Cloudflare service and dedicated database. Preserve portable software, SQLite-compatible data, consent and export history for a future move to local island servers. Do not migrate hosting or replace the service identity as an incidental redesign choice.
- Do not describe planned systems as working. The public notice publishing/synchronisation network and C-Hour ledger/personal agent are not implemented here. Production organiser access remains locked until its real access configuration is completed and verified.
- Run checks appropriate to the changed files. Check shared navigation and local links/anchors after content changes; verify mobile layouts and the real participation flow when affected. Keep private records, credentials and local state out of the public build and Git.

See [the brief](docs/REBUILD-BRIEF.md) for page roles and original sources. Use Australian English and preserve original source editions.
