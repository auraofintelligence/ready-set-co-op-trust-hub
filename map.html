    How this map works

    Find a place, add something missing, or suggest a correction. New information is checked before it appears on the public map.

    Include useful local details: an unmarked bus stop, a nearby landmark, a seat in the shade or who can use a facility. A listing describes a place and does not imply cooperative membership.

    The place records are held in our local register, initially operated by Strange But True. You can download the published records and keep a copy. Map background data is supplied by OpenStreetMap.
