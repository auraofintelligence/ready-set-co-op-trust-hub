Included above · one page

Your first enquiry.

Start on the Take part page with a short enquiry about what you would like to do. You can leave your details, choose what interests you and keep a receipt after it is saved.

  • Share your interest: leave contact details, choose what interests you and decide what contact or sharing you agree to.
  • Receive confirmation: after the enquiry is saved, the form shows your reference.
  • Keep a receipt: download a copy of your enquiry and reference from that same screen.

Luke then follows up about a possible next step. The form does not make you a member or employee. Formal membership, member accounts and preparation for a specific role need their own arrangements.
