C-Hours · voluntary contribution

One verified human hour. A contribution worth recognising.

One C-hour recognises one verified hour of voluntary human contribution. It has no monetary equivalent. Communities choose their own thanks and reciprocal opportunities.

Room for what people already give.

Care, mentoring, helping a neighbour, sharing knowledge and supporting a community activity can all be part of this exploration. The description preserves what someone did, learned and helped make possible.

A record you help shape.

The proposed approach uses a conversation and sources the participant chooses. A personal agent could prepare a record for that person to check, correct and decide whether to share. Private information remains under their control.

Paid time stays in paid-work records. Machine time and machine output have their own records. C-Hours are a proposed recognition system; this website does not yet run a C-Hour ledger or personal agent.

Learn it. Improve it. Pass it on.

  1. Choose a useful task

    Agree the purpose, whether it is paid or voluntary, and what support is needed.

  2. Work with a mentor

    Try the task with suitable tools and a person available to help check the result.

  3. Leave a useful handover

    Keep the instructions and learning that another person can use, with permission for anything shared.
