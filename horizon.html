The connection to everyday work

Start with skills people can use.

People learning to repair equipment, document a project or work with digital tools can also help investigate new ideas. The connection is practical learning: try a small task, check the result and share what was learned.

Ready SETCO's immediate focus remains local events, training, work and useful shared services. As members, we would decide whether any longer-term research becomes a cooperative project.

Before an idea becomes a project

Questions worth asking together.

These are questions for anyone exploring an earlier research idea.

PurposeWho would it help?

Explain a real need and ask the people affected whether the idea is useful.

EvidenceWhat has actually been tested?

Separate a drawing or proposal from demonstrated results and an operating service.

ResponsibilityWho would do the work?

Agree the team, costs, permissions, training and ongoing care before committing.
