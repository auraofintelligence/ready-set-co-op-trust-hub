Ready SETCO is being developed as a locally governed cooperative. The invitation is to help decide what is useful, what is affordable and what we choose to build together.

Local choice

A proposal earns its place.

The first proposed shared build is the Straddie Noticeboard Network. Events, training, media and practical support offer ways to begin working together. Members will choose the cooperative's commitments.

Luke's research provides material to consider. A project appearing on this website or in a connected library does not mean the cooperative has adopted it, funded it or secured a partner.

A useful decision record

  • What is proposed and who would benefit?
  • Who has a say and who holds responsibility?
  • What will it cost, and who has agreed to help?
  • What was decided, by whom and when?
  • What happened, and when do we review it?

These are proposed foundations. The detailed decision process belongs in the rules members develop.

From the seed to shared responsibility

An agreed handover, with a clear record.

Strange But True begins the practical work as Luke's sole trader business. A registered Ready SETCO can take on agreed services when its people, registrations and arrangements are ready.

  1. Agree what moves

    Identify the service, assets, ongoing costs, contracts and people responsible. Record the cooperative's acceptance.

  2. Respect each person's permission

    The participation form asks separately about future sharing with the registered cooperative. Contact details move only through the agreed consent and handover process.

  3. Keep the history

    Record the original operator, the receiving legal entity and the effective date. Existing work and obligations keep their proper history.

  4. Support the new team

    Pass on instructions, access, training and maintenance knowledge so the service can continue under its agreed responsibility.

Clear records

Recognise each kind of contribution.

Financial records track wages, operating costs and investment. C-Hours recognise verified voluntary human contribution without a monetary equivalent. Project records describe the results and lessons.

People choose what to share from their private learning and contribution records. Machinery and AI output are recorded separately from human time.
