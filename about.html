The idea behind Ready SETCO

A minimum viable community.

For a fair go in the age of superintelligence. Enough shared organisation and capability for people to learn, earn, participate and get useful things done together.

Help shape itMembers & decisions

Begin close to home

Useful support, chosen locally.

Ready Sustainable Employment and Training Cooperative starts as a proposal on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island. Events, training, notices, news, media, grants and compliance support give people practical ways to work together.

The first proposed shared build is the Straddie Noticeboard Network, connecting residents, businesses, community groups and tourists. Everyday information and disaster readiness belong in the same community network.

Strange But True is the initial sole trader operator. Cooperative formation and the transfer of responsibilities follow their own agreed steps.

What is the minimum?

  • People who want to participate.
  • A way to make local decisions.
  • Useful shared information.
  • Opportunities to learn and do paid work.
  • Practical support and accountable records.

The people involved decide what these foundations should support.

A fractal pattern

Repeat the foundations. Keep the local choices.

The same practical pattern could be adapted across the Southern Moreton Bay Islands, Redlands and communities around Australia. Each place brings its own people, needs, culture and priorities.

Locally governed

Each community chooses its services, responsibilities and projects. A postcode is a useful way to find people; an island or another locally meaningful boundary may fit the work better.

Connected as peers

Communities can share tools, training, lessons and purchasing opportunities through agreed relationships. The pattern leaves room for cooperatives and other locally suitable organisations.

Improve through use

Try something useful, document what happens, change what needs changing and help the next community begin with better knowledge.

People around shared capability

Grow into the infrastructure we need.

Installing the Missing Middle proposes community computing, useful heat and pathways into local cooperative ownership. Ready SETCO can help develop the people, learning and coordination around a future installation.

Operating or acquiring equipment would follow an agreed handover, with named owners, service duties, training, maintenance and renewal costs. Local participation can begin before a server arrives.

Built to travel

This first service is hosted on Cloudflare. The aim is to keep the records and software portable so local servers can become an option when the island has suitable facilities and an agreed operating team.
