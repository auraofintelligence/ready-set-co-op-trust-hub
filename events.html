Events & experiences · Minjerribah

Help bring an event to Straddie.

A workshop, a music night or a film under the stars brings people together and gives local businesses and crews a reason to work together. Luke Hayes is inviting locals to help plan events like these as a starting point for Ready SETCO, the proposed community-owned cooperative.

Bring an idea for something you would like to attend, a venue or service you could offer, or an interest in helping welcome guests. Together, the people involved can choose a manageable first event and work out what it needs.

Tell us your event interestSee the starting ideas

Planning stage. Dates, venues, prices and bookings will be published when an event is confirmed.

Starting ideas

Learn, explore, listen and watch.

These are a few possibilities to shape with local hosts, businesses, performers and people who want to take part.

AI workshops & conferences

Practical sessions could help locals and visitors use artificial intelligence (AI) for writing, research and creative work. Luke Hayes would teach through his business Strange But True, with a guest-services team helping with arrivals, bookings, hospitality and visitor questions.

Island experiences

Visitors attending an event could also explore the island through local tours and services. Interested operators and hosts would agree what they offer, their prices and responsibilities before anything is advertised.

Music & creative gatherings

Bring performers, production crews and audiences together. Local stories, music and conversations can make an event part of a longer creative relationship.

Outdoor cinema & screenings

Explore shared film nights with appropriate screening rights, equipment, access, weather arrangements and venue permission. Media training can grow around the production work.

The work around the event

A good welcome starts with local people.

Knowing the island, helping someone find their way, preparing a room and keeping the day running all matter. An event could bring those strengths together and give people a chance to learn alongside someone experienced.

Each role would have clear duties and support, with pay or voluntary arrangements agreed before anyone commits.

From idea to invitation

Make each gathering ready to welcome people.

  1. Shape the activity

    Choose the audience, purpose, scope, organiser and local people who want to take part.

  2. Prepare the details

    Confirm the venue, budget, access, staffing, permissions, insurance and arrangements for changes or disruption.

  3. Publish checked information

    Share the confirmed date, booking route and contact details, then keep changes visible through public notices.
