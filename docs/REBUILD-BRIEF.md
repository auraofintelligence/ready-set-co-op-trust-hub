# Ready SETCO rebuild brief

Accepted direction from Luke, 19 November 2026. This records the public rebuild's purpose and limits; it is not a cooperative decision or adopted constitution.

## The framing

**A minimum viable community for a fair go in the age of superintelligence.**

“Minimum viable” means enough shared organisation and capability for people to learn, earn, participate and get useful things done together. “Fractal” describes how communities can repeat the foundations, share learning and cooperate as peers while choosing their own priorities. Begin on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island, with room for locally shaped participation across the Southern Moreton Bay Islands, Redlands and other places. A postcode is a way to discover connections, not a prescribed cooperative boundary or guaranteed installation.

## The first shared build

The **Straddie Noticeboard Network** connects people forming the cooperative, local businesses, residents, community groups and tourists. It is the first proposed shared build. The current Noticeboard website is a prototype with researched examples; inclusion does not mean an organisation has opted in. Its browser builder prepares a notice, rather than publishing or synchronising an operating network.

The latest **Minjerribah Screen & Media Network** is the media gateway. **Aura Disaster Resilience** is embedded in the everyday information network's disruption and recovery design. Preserve the issuer, issue time, receipt/check time, expiry, connection state and distinction between current, cached and demonstration information. Responsible official services retain their authority.

## Participation and stewardship

Strange But True starts as the sole trader operator. Cooperative registration, formal membership and transfer of services require their own agreed process. Keep the original operator and consent history when responsibilities move; name the receiving legal entity and effective date.

C-Hour is non-monetary recognition of verified voluntary human contribution. Paid work, investment, machine time and output, and community outcomes have separate records. Participants choose what to share from their person-controlled private records. Cultural knowledge and information about Country retain their custodial authority and permissions.

Installing the Missing Middle provides a future infrastructure connection: people, learning and coordination can grow before an installation. Operation or acquisition follows agreed ownership, duties, commissioning, training, maintenance and renewal arrangements. Do not imply completed installations, secured property, funding or member adoption.

## Design and delivery

Preserve highlighted purple, blue, yellow and green from Strange But True, including the new tide favicon. Use a mobile-first layout, at least 16px body text, 44px interaction targets, accessible focus and navigation, safe-area spacing and reduced-motion support. Load no external map until chosen; request no automatic geolocation. Keep direct map links and meaningful content usable without JavaScript.

The inline Strange but True brand link follows Luke's supplied Facebook Profile and Facebook Cover - Wide Coast images: “Strange” purple, “but” cyan, “True” yellow/gold, and “Tech, Art and Ideas That'll Help” green. Keep the full phrase bold and linked to the Strange but True website. A dark backing preserves the bright reference colours on a pale page.

Retain the existing Cloudflare hosting and portable software/data approach. Local island servers are a future hosting option with an agreed team and facilities.

This rebuild ships public information pages, geographic map exploration and the existing backed participation form. It does **not** ship a live notice publishing/synchronisation network, C-Hour ledger, personal agent or completed production organiser sign-in. Keep organiser routes closed until access is actually configured and verified. Earlier Trust Hub, Ballow Road and horizon pages remain marked as earlier research.
