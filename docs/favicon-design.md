# Ready SETCO tide mark

Created 19 November 2026 using the built-in image generation tool. This is a new
AI-generated brand emblem, with a targeted colour edit to follow the user's
Strange But True palette instruction. It contains no letters or initials.

The flowing open tide circle suggests island life and shared momentum. Purple,
blue and green strands surround a yellow sun or seed on a midnight navy rounded
tile. The small area outside the rounded corners is transparent.

## Palette

- Purple: `#8f4dff`
- Blue: `#5fd8ff`
- Yellow: `#ffd166`
- Green: `#62f6a5`
- Midnight navy: `#101020`

Generated colour shading approximates these reference values rather than
claiming every pixel uses an exact flat token.

## Original generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: a square favicon and app icon for Ready
Sustainable Employment and Training Cooperative, a coastal community organisation
on Minjerribah. Create one beautiful original emblem, not a mockup or a design
sheet. Subject: two broad, graceful turquoise and sea-glass aqua tide forms
flowing together into an open, almost circular embrace, with one small warm
golden sun/seed nestled in the open upper space. The flowing forms should suggest
shared momentum, ocean, learning and community without using letters or generic
connected network dots. A distinctive, balanced silhouette with generous
negative space; beautifully resolved bold shapes that remain recognisable at
16px and 32px. Style: premium contemporary coastal emblem, crisp clean edges,
minimal dimensional depth, restrained luminous colour, no fine lines or small
decorations. Background: deep midnight navy-purple square with softly rounded
corners; make it an edge-to-edge finished icon with only a small safe inset around
the emblem. Palette: saturated turquoise, pale aqua highlights, one warm gold
accent, midnight navy-purple. No text, initials, RS, letters, numbers, watermark,
border, typography, photoreal scene, objects, mockup, gradients outside the icon,
or multiple alternatives. Deliver the single square finished mark.

## Final targeted edit prompt

Edit this finished favicon only by changing the palette and corner transparency.
Preserve the exact distinctive flowing tide-circle emblem, its broad bold shapes,
circular yellow sun, negative spaces, composition and rounded square tile. The
user's essential brand colours are ALL FOUR: vivid Strange But True purple
#8f4dff, bright sky blue #5fd8ff, warm yellow #ffd166 and luminous green #62f6a5,
against midnight navy #101020. Make the upper-left outer flowing strand
unmistakably vivid purple, the middle inner wave unmistakably bright blue, the
large lower-right tide unmistakably luminous green, and the sun unmistakably warm
yellow. All four colours should be distinct clear highlights, not variations of
teal. Use bold clean colour areas with only subtle dimensional richness,
exceptional 16px/32px clarity and crisp edges. Keep the midnight navy rounded
tile; the small area outside its rounded corners must be genuinely transparent.
No new shapes, letters, words, RS, watermark, mockup, border, background scene or
extra decorations.
