- 9 Ballow Road exterior: front, side, entry, access ramp, car park, rear access and bay-view context.

- Co-working table: laptops, notebooks, forms, tea, printed checklists and public/private folder labels.

- Gear care board: microphone, phone clamp, tripod, projector, chargers, sign-out card and repair note.

- Grant evidence table: attendance note, timeline, photo folder, budget note and support quote list.

- Shared table or food security context: produce, meal prep, recipe note or pantry story, with health and cultural care.

## Capture Rules

- Do not film private screens, paperwork, addresses, children, cultural material, vulnerable people, expensive gear locations or informal conversations unless permission is clear.
- Shoot one vertical version and one wide version for each important story.
- Use public-safe alt text notes while capturing, not weeks later.
- Refresh property and grant facts close to publication.
