# From a local map to the Web3 Sensorium

Working implementation brief, 19 November 2026. This records Luke's direction for the current build; it is not an adopted cooperative policy or an assertion that the wider systems exist.

**Current scope stays simple and usable across ages:** Ready SETCO leads with events, training, notices/news/media, grants and compliance support. The map helps people find, add and correct local places and facilities, with simple human review and export. The wider Sensorium is an endpoint constraint for compatible records, not an instruction to build personal/home/bioregion portals, a governance engine or a team task manager into this app.

## 1. The endpoint and the first useful slice

Ready SETCO is a proposed minimum viable community for a fair go in the age of superintelligence. Its local map is one view into a fractal Web3 Sensorium: digital twins of self, home, shared projects, communities and bioregions that can help people understand their world and govern fairly. Communities can overlap and choose suitable arrangements. A postcode can help find connections without becoming a compulsory ownership or governance boundary.

Luke has said that none of the earlier map attempts meets the desired quality. Their designs and components are not endorsed templates for this rebuild. Read them for evidence, lessons, corrections and interfaces; build one coherent experience around the current purpose. The first slice must let people record real businesses, informal and marked bus stops, shelters, seats, picnic equipment, toilets, clubs, beaches and other useful things, then improve the records together.

The map, place list, noticeboard and later twin should read shared records with stable identities. They should not each become separate, incompatible catalogues. Public map pins are an authorised public view; a person's private self or home environment is a separate, person-controlled space.

## 2. Private foundations, chosen relationships

The current C-Hour Sensorium chapter defines **L0 as the person and their controlled private environment**, including information, devices, spaces and agent interactions. Its L0-to-L3 example is explicitly not a fixed four-level hierarchy, legal classification or ranking. Different groups may overlap, and wider participation grants no automatic access to private information. A new purpose requires a new agreement. Participants need understandable ways to inspect, correct, pause, retain, share and withdraw information, with honest limits on deletion or copies already shared.

P4A's current architecture uses a compatible private foundation and flexible wider scales. It is context for the design, not a reason to import political membership, political permissions or a national parent authority into Ready SETCO. A place owner, record contributor, cultural custodian, service operator and cooperative decision-maker may be different people with different powers.

Older Web3 Sensorium and Sovereign Space Builder specifications explore sovereign nodes, credentials, replicated records, fractal organisations and custodial access. Preserve their source editions. Current human direction and the current C-Hour treatment govern where older fixed tiers, speculative token language or reputation-weighted governance differ. C-Hours remain non-monetary records of voluntary human contribution, separate from wages, money, investment, machine activity and outcomes.

## 3. The minimum record and working loop

The first implementation should support **add an observation → check its source → publish or correct the public record → export its identity and history**. Keep the phone form brief: the feature, its position and useful local details, with optional evidence. Populate technical metadata in the background; retain clear submission and publication states.

Keep the following concepts separate, even where the first database represents them simply:

- **Scope and responsibility:** identify the public collection and responsible people or organisation. Keep the model compatible with separately controlled spaces later; private Aura/home records and portals are outside this build.
- **Place or asset:** a stable identity for the thing; category and description; location or geometry with its source and precision; relationships such as a seat within a park or a shelter beside a bus stopping point. An address or precinct centroid is not automatically the asset's position.
- **Observation and evidence:** what someone observed, when, how and about which thing; contributor/source reference; GPS accuracy or map-selected position; evidence links and confidence. Existence, present condition, access and current business operation are different claims. Keep observation, proposal, simulation and uncertainty distinguishable.
- **Publication and revision:** original report, corrections, reviewer decisions, responsible publisher, timestamps, version and withdrawal state. Preserve disagreements and history without presenting withdrawn or unverified data as current public fact. A publication permission does not establish ownership of the physical asset or authority over Country.
- **Portable handover:** export public records in useful standard forms, and restrict private operational exports separately. Stable IDs and retained provenance should survive a move from the initial Cloudflare service to agreed island infrastructure.

Existing source corrections matter. Earlier island work records misplaced geocoder points, removed facilities and disputed locations. Researched organisations and old map pins do not establish current participation, opening status or verified condition. Preserve those cautions and source attribution; do not automatically republish every old record. Cultural authority and permissions remain with the relevant custodians. Private addresses, restricted knowledge and contributor details must not leak through a public export, map or repository history.

## 4. A local team that can take over

Develop small work packages with named responsibility, a practice example, review and handover. These are human training/operating roles, not a new task-management app or appointments already made:

| Role | First useful package | Handover evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mapper / contributor | Record a missing feature, its position, source and uncertainty. | A saved observation another person can understand and revisit. |
| Verifier | Check a location or condition against field evidence and appropriate sources. | A dated finding, remaining uncertainty and a reason for correction. |
| Place steward | Keep an agreed place collection current and handle corrections. | Named scope, access arrangements and a review queue with continuity. |
| Scan / model builder | Capture an authorised asset and attach evidence, measurements or models. | Capture provenance, units, accuracy and permission, linked to the stable asset ID. |
| Digital custodian / operator | Maintain access, backups, exports, recovery and hosting. | A tested restore, operating notes and authority to perform the agreed duties. |

People can learn across roles, with mentoring and paid work where agreed. Voluntary contribution retains its separate recognition. Train a second person to complete each repeatable package; record the receiving team or entity, scope, consent and effective date when responsibilities transfer. Luke's initial work should produce teachable capability and an accountable handover rather than permanent dependence on him.

Publication review handles data quality and permissions. It is not democratic governance by itself. Cooperative admission, member decisions, policy changes, disputes and collective resource choices need their own agreed processes and legitimate participants.

## 5. Stages and source foundations

At the start of this map-input task, the Ready SETCO preview provides public information pages, geographic exploration and a backed expression-of-interest form. The richer place/observation workflow is being developed. Production organiser access remains closed until actually configured and verified. Separate noticeboard publishing, personal agents and a C-Hour ledger are not delivered by the current site.

Growth compatibility means stable records can later link authorised photographs, scans, asset models and twin views. It does not expand the present app. Private spaces, federation, peer-to-peer/offline synchronisation, credentials, CRDT merging, blockchain governance and automatic 3D modelling require separate work and verification. Cloudflare is the initial hosting choice; portable records and clear ownership support a later local handover.
