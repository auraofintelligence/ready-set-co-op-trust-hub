Create the first webmaster account

This one-time setup connects your private webmaster account to the existing Cloudflare service.

One Cloudflare setting, then your account

  1. The key stays in this browser until you copy it. Keep this page open.

  2. In Cloudflare, open Workers & Pages → ready-setco → Settings. Find the Worker's Variables and Secrets section above Builds, then choose Add → Secret, use the name READY_AUTH_SECRET, paste the copied key as its value, then choose Deploy.

  3. Then complete your details below and choose your own password.

Keep the secret in Cloudflare after setup. The first webmaster claim closes automatically when it succeeds.
