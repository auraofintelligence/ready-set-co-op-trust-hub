Grants & project support

A good idea needs a workable plan.

Bring the local need, the people and the evidence together. Ready SETCO's proposed support helps communities prepare for funding and keep delivery organised.

Bring a projectExplore the Grants Lab

Start with the purpose

What would this make possible?

A screening, learning programme, useful equipment or shared noticeboard can start with a small, specific brief. Members choose which proposals the cooperative takes forward.

  1. Describe the need

    Who would benefit, what have they asked for, and what is the smallest useful result?

  2. Name the applicant and delivery team

    Record who can apply, who will hold the funds and who will do the work. Confirm each organisation's agreement before naming it as a partner.

  3. Build a realistic budget

    Include people, access, equipment, insurance, permissions, maintenance and reporting. Keep quotes and assumptions with the figures.

  4. Check the actual funding round

    Use the funder's current guidelines for eligibility, deadlines, permitted costs and assessment. A possible match is a lead to investigate.

Build evidence as you go

Keep one useful project folder.

Bring together the brief, decision record, budget, quotes, permissions, delivery dates and the person responsible for each next step. Record what happened and what people learned.

Photos, attendance and participant stories need appropriate permission. Publish an agreed summary, with private working material kept separately.

Before submitting

  • A clear local purpose and agreed scope.
  • A confirmed applicant and authorised sign-off.
  • Evidence for the need and budget.
  • Delivery responsibilities and reporting dates.
  • Current guidelines and a final human review.
